01 · Encounter: Spot the Problem

Read the two situations. With a partner, describe the problem and suggest what someone might say first.

🚌 Everyday life · Missed bus

You and your friend are going to a concert. The bus is delayed and the concert starts in 20 minutes.

Discuss: What's wrong? Is there enough time? What could you do?

💻 Customer support · Website error

A customer cannot complete a payment on a website. The page keeps showing an error message.

Discuss: How can you describe the issue? What should the agent say next?

Compare your language

  1. Which words did you use to describe the problem?
  2. How would your tone change with a customer?
  3. What is more helpful: only naming the problem, or offering a next step?
Notice: Effective problem solving follows a simple sequence: name the issue → consider the time → suggest a solution → confirm understanding.

02 · Clarify: Problem-Solving Expressions

Explore four expression categories. Click each card to reveal a contextual example, then click again to return.

Expression bank · 16 interactive cards

From problem to next step

1 · Identify

“Something's wrong with the link.”

2 · Explain time pressure

“We're running late.”

3 · Offer an idea

“I've got it! Let's use another link.”

4 · Confirm understanding

“I see! That should work.”

Useful professional extensions: “Let me check that for you.” · “Here's what we can do.” · “I understand the issue.” These are additional phrases, not replacements for the original expression bank.

Speaking drill: In pairs, create a four-line conversation using one phrase from each category.

03 · Remember: Choose the Best Expression

Choose the expression that fits each situation. Check your answers to receive explanations, then retry as needed.

Speaking bridge: Choose one quiz situation and turn your answer into a two-line conversation.

04 · Internalize: Two Problems, Two Conversations

Work in pairs. Complete both scenarios and switch roles. Each dialogue must use at least two expressions from different categories, plus a realistic next step.

🏠 Round 1 · Everyday life (7 min)

You and your roommate are planning dinner. The oven is not working, and your guests will arrive soon.

Reveal guiding questions and phrases
  1. What exactly isn't working?
  2. How much time do you have?
  3. Could you cook something else or order food?
  4. Useful phrases: “Something's wrong.” · “We don't have time.” · “I have an idea!”

🎧 Round 2 · Customer support (8 min)

A customer is trying to reset a password, but the reset email hasn't arrived. The customer needs access today.

Reveal guiding questions and phrases
  1. How will the agent acknowledge the issue?
  2. How can the agent ask for more information?
  3. What next step could the agent suggest without making an unsupported promise?
  4. Useful phrases: “We have an issue.” · “Let me check that for you.” · “Here's what we can do.” · “I see!”

Partner feedback

Did your partner identify the problem clearly, use appropriate expressions, offer a practical step, and show understanding?

05 · Fluent Use: Problem-Solving Challenge

Work with a partner. Choose a surprise scenario, then create a natural 45–60 second mini dialogue. Do not read a script.

🎲 Surprise scenario

Press “New scenario” to reveal your challenge.

Your conversation should include

  1. A clear description of the problem.
  2. At least two expressions from different categories.
  3. A helpful suggestion or next step.
  4. A response that confirms understanding.
